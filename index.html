The salon

A calm, beautiful space for the work you care about

Affinity Luxury Salon sits on Madhya Marg in Sector 9C, near Grewal Eye Institute. Our senior artists cover everything from a sharp gents' cut to a full bridal look, with skincare, spa and nail art under the same roof.

Every appointment starts with a conversation about what you want, so the result suits your hair, your skin and your day.

  • Hair artistryCuts, colour and styling for men and women
  • Bridal studioBridal makeup and complete bridal packages
  • Skin and spaFacials and 60 or 90 minute body spa
  • By appointmentBook ahead so your artist is ready for you
